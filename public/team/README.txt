Team & Mentors Photos Directory
=================================
You can drop team member photos (PNG, JPG, JPEG, WebP) directly into this folder (/public/team/).

To link a member to their picture:
1. Put the photo here, e.g. /public/team/arindam-mukherjee.jpg
2. In /public/data/team.json, set the "avatarUrl": "/team/arindam-mukherjee.jpg"
3. You can also edit names, designations ("post"), departments ("departmentOrBatch"), emails, and social links in /public/data/team.json.
